3. Device permissions

The app requests certain permissions, only when they are needed:

  • Camera: scan barcodes and photograph your meals.
  • Photo library: choose a profile, post, recipe or coach-chat photo.
  • Motion / pedometer: estimate your steps, distance and calories.
  • Calendar: add your planned meals and workouts to your calendar, if you ask for it.
  • Notifications: reminders for planned meals.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your phone settings.

4. Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

With your consent, TrackScore reads your steps, distance and active calories from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS, or from your device's sensors. This data is used only to display your dashboard and estimate your calorie expenditure; the source (Apple Health) is identified in the interface. The app does not write data to Apple Health. Your health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for research without separate consent, in accordance with Apple's HealthKit rules.

5. AI photo analysis (meal scan)

The "AI Scan" feature lets you photograph a meal to estimate its calories and macronutrients. The photo is sent, only at analysis time, to our AI provider (Anthropic) through a secure server. It is used to produce the estimate and is not otherwise exploited by TrackScore. This feature is optional: nothing is analyzed unless you take or choose a photo yourself.

6. Coach space and messaging

Linking with a coach is voluntary: the link only exists if you enter the code your coach gave you. Once linked:

  • your coach can view your workout sessions (programs, exercises, loads) and an aggregated daily summary of your calories in and out — never the details of your food journal;
  • they can send you advice and assign programs;
  • you share a private chat (text and photos). Chat photos are stored in a private space, accessible only to the two participants through time-limited secure links;
  • you can end the link at any time (from settings or the conversation menu): the coach immediately loses access to your data.
