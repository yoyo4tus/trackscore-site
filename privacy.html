2. Device permissions

We request permissions only when needed: Camera (barcode and meal photos), Photo library (profile/post/recipe photos), Motion/pedometer (steps, distance, calories), Calendar (optional export of planned meals/sessions), Notifications (meal reminders). You can revoke them anytime in your phone settings.

3. Health data (Apple Health / Health Connect)

With your consent, TrackScore reads steps, distance and calories to display your dashboard. The app does not write data to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties for marketing.

5. International transfers

Some processors may handle data outside the EU (notably Anthropic, a US company, when AI photo analysis is enabled). Such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses). Database hosting is provided by Supabase; the hosting region is available on request.

6. Legal basis

Account and activity data are processed to perform the service you request. Health data (weight, height, BMI, steps, calories, activity), a special category under GDPR Article 9, is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, collected at sign-up and, on iOS, via the Apple Health authorization.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact trackscore.admin@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the French CNIL.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account directly in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. All your data is then permanently erased. You may also email us to request deletion.

9. Security

Data access is protected by authentication and database-level Row Level Security: each user can only access their own private data.

10. Minimum age

In France the age of digital consent is 15. You must be at least 15 to use TrackScore; below that age, consent from a holder of parental authority is required.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest revision.
